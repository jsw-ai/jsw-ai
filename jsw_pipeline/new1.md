## 1. Stills: Seedream single-reference edit

- <b>Tool:</b> Higgsfield MCP <code>user-Higgsfield</code> → <code>generate_image</code>, or <code>generate_image_batch</code> with a literal <code>requests</code> array of <code>{index, params}</code>.
- <b>Model / params:</b> <code>{"model":"seedream_v5_pro","prompt":"…","aspect_ratio":"9:16","resolution":"2k","medias":[{"value":"&lt;media_id&gt;","role":"image_references"}]}</code>. ONE image reference = one approved v4C final from the model's row (single-ref "Edit this exact photo"). 9:16 for video starts and full-frame exports, 3:4 for feed stills (3:4 2k = 1760x2352 PNG). Cost 2.5 cr/image; <code>count:2</code> bills 2.5 per image.
- <b>Upload a ref:</b> <code>media_upload</code> → <code>curl -X PUT</code> to the presigned URL with headers <code>Content-Type</code> <b>and</b> <code>If-None-Match: *</code> → <code>media_confirm</code> (media\_ids list, one call per type). Media ids stay valid across calls.
- <b>Prompt template</b> (valentina\_oct22/work/still\_prompts.json, batch1a.json): <code>Edit this exact photo: same woman, same pose and &lt;framing&gt; framing, arms relaxed and fully in frame. Change her outfit to &lt;outfit, modest/high neckline&gt;, and change the setting to &lt;empty scene&gt;, &lt;light&gt;.</code> + face-freeze line + the model's <b>locked body line, word for word</b> + "No tattoos…" + expression line + "Soft daylight evenly lit on her face from the front, no side light, no harsh shadows." + skin block ("Unretouched natural skin with fine visible pores, light natural skin texture, dry matte skin, no shine, no sheen, no glow, no highlighter, no airbrushing.") + "No other people anywhere in frame. No text, no signs, no logos. Realistic candid phone photo, vertical 9:16, with a band of empty background above her head."
- <b>Outputs:</b> job ids saved to <code>work/w&lt;n&gt;.json</code> (<code>[{index, job_id}]</code>) and <code>work/jobs&lt;n&gt;.tsv</code> (<code>index, name, job_id</code>); download to <code>raw/&lt;name&gt;.png</code>. Download command/endpoint: <b>TBD</b> (not in the files).
- <b>Pass:</b> job status completed, PNG present, then step 2 and step 3. <b>Fail:</b> status <code>nsfw</code> (refunded; rephrase neutrally / higher neckline / swap ref once or twice, then stop, never loop or bypass); background people or text (reroll); see Common failures.
- <b>Rules:</b> never chain edits (re-anchor on the approved original or the passing job id); mid framing (waist-up, head-to-hips) holds identity best, close face-large and full-body wides drift; hair down, no ponytail; empty scenes; even frontal light (sunset/side light narrows the face).
- <b>Common failures → fixes:</b> narrower/older face on fresh multi-ref stills → single-ref edit of one approved final. "Soft closed-mouth smirk" narrowed the face (6/7 at .69-.78) → "same natural warm smile as in the photo" (.80-.86), let Kling soften it. Night scenes from daylight stills drift → get a passing daylight still in the same room, then edit THAT to night. Fuller-bust wording + deep scoop = too much cleavage and moderation blocks → modest crew/high scoop neck. Arm sliver at the frame edge becomes a tattoo-like patch → keep arms fully in or out of frame.

## 3. Identity gate (face gate)

- <b>Files:</b> <code>/workspace/hf-test/zen/posting/identity_gate.py</code>, <code>identity_packs.json</code> (key = the model's identity\_pack key). Every-frame video gate: <code>valentina_oct22/work/vgate.py</code> (pack key hard-coded <code>'valentina'</code>; a script must parameterize it) or the multi-file variant <code>camille_oct22/work/vgate_multi.py</code>.
- <b>Pre-check:</b> <code>free -g</code> "available" ≥ 3 and no other gate process running (<code>pgrep -f "identity_gate|vgate"</code>); otherwise wait. One gate at a time.
- <b>Stills:</b> <code>/workspace/.face-venv/bin/python /workspace/hf-test/zen/posting/identity_gate.py img --model &lt;pack&gt; --source seedream --body &lt;files…&gt; --json &lt;out.json&gt; --log /workspace/hf-test/zen/posting/reject_log.jsonl</code>. Exit 0 = all pass. JSON per file: <code>pass_, reasons[], score_centroid, score_best, body, body_pass, visual_check_required</code>. Thumbs with burned text: add <code>--no-ocr</code> or use a clean frame.
- <b>Video (every frame):</b> <code>/workspace/.face-venv/bin/python &lt;batch&gt;/work/vgate.py &lt;clip.mp4&gt; [step] [out.json]</code> (step 1 = every frame; default out <code>&lt;clip&gt;_gate.json</code> with <code>frames, scored, median, min, below70, below78, per_frame[[t,score]], PASS</code>). Ship a per-frame CSV <code>final/&lt;export&gt;_gate.csv</code> with header <code>t_s,score</code> (CSV writer script: TBD). Scale 1244x1664 Kling pro output to 622x832 before gating (same score, avoids OOM). Do NOT use <code>identity_gate.py video</code> thresholds (median .58 / floor .50 / 8 frames): retired.
- <b>Pass bars:</b> final still ≥ <b>0.78</b> centroid; Kling start still ≥ <b>0.85</b>; video/final export <b>median ≥ 0.78 and no scored frame &lt; 0.70</b>, every frame scored, on the FINAL encode (and on the raw clip; leave ~0.05 headroom over .70 on raw). Treat ±0.01 around .78 as borderline. Three-quarter faces are scored; only true profile/turned-away frames are unscored.
- <b>Threshold note:</b> identity\_gate's <code>seedream_centroid</code> threshold defaults to 0.78 (<code>THRESH</code> in identity\_gate.py; no CLI flag), but Sienna's still bar is 0.79, so a script must take the threshold from each model's settings row and compare <code>score_centroid</code> against it itself. Also note non-Seedream sources still use the old <code>centroid=0.72 / best_ref=0.74</code> in THRESH, so always pass <code>--source seedream</code> for Seedream stills or enforce the row's bar in the script.
- <b>Mandatory visual check:</b> a score alone is not a pass. Build a same-scale side-by-side (4-6 stills/frames incl. mid and late frames next to the approved refs; e.g. <code>valentina_tiktok_oct19/qc.sh &lt;n&gt; &lt;base&gt;</code>: 360x480 tiles, frames at 0.1/1/2/3/4/4.9 s) and write a face + body + hair note. Every Reviewer submission carries <code>VISUAL: checked</code>.
- <b>Common failures → fixes:</b> OOM-killed gate → wait for ≥3 GB, run alone, downscale. Laughs, blown-kiss pucker, grimaces, wide brow raises, glances score .46-.74 → avoid or cut. Export drops: re-encode/drawtext/noise move frames ±.03; punch-in zoom, xfade blends and text over the forehead cost score → hard cuts, no zoom on borderline spans, text above the head. Gate scores are inflated when refs = gate refs: Samuel's/Reviewer's eye is final.

## 4. Kling 3.0 low-motion clip with the model's Element

- <b>Element:</b> from the model's row. To create one: upload 3 approved finals (media\_upload → PUT → media\_confirm) → <code>manage_reference_elements</code> create (instant, free). 400 "Reference element is not ready" → resubmit a few seconds later (free).
- <b>Job params</b> (valentina\_oct22/work/batch\_k67.json): <code>{"model":"kling3_0","prompt":"&lt;&lt;&lt;ELEMENT_ID&gt;&gt;&gt; …","medias":[{"value":"&lt;start media_id&gt;","role":"start_image"}],"duration":5,"aspect_ratio":"9:16","mode":"pro"|"std","sound":false,"declined_preset_id":"&lt;id from the preset notice&gt;"}</code>. Cost sound off: std 7.5 cr/5 s (1.5 cr/s), pro 8.75 cr/5 s; sound on 10. ~5-8 min render. Std 3:4 sources come out 828x1108; check the output size before building.
- <b>Start still:</b> an approved v4C final used as-is, or a Kling frame that scores ≥ .89 (kB4 f0 → kB6 median .819). Must gate ≥ <b>0.85</b>; even frontal light, modest neckline, arms fully in or out, no tattoo, closed-mouth soft smile.
- <b>Prompt template</b> (k\_D1.txt, batch\_k67.json): <code>&lt;&lt;&lt;ELEMENT&gt;&gt;&gt; &lt;place&gt;, framing exactly as in the first frame. &lt;one low motion&gt;. Head stays upright and level, facing the camera the whole time, soft closed-mouth smile that stays the same, eyes open, natural blinks only. No head tilt, no laughing, no turning. Keep the same face as the first frame, keep skin texture, matte skin, no sheen. Keep body proportions identical to the first frame. &lt;locked body line&gt; She is alone. Camera locked, no zoom. No text. No speech, silent.</code>
- <b>Output:</b> <code>vid/k&lt;id&gt;.mp4</code>; log <code>index, name, job_id, start</code> in <code>work/jobs_k.tsv</code>.
- <b>Pass:</b> vgate raw median ≥ .78, min ≥ .70 (aim ≥ .75), plus visual body/outfit check. <b>Fail → fix:</b> "IN THE DARK" preset notice → resubmit with <code>declined_preset_id</code> (free). Start below .85 → likely drift (kD1 median .712) → better start. "Almost still" wording made it worse (kB7 min .637). Hand transitions (palm to lens) → banned, use ffmpeg cuts. Eyes drop when a cup is in frame → eyes-on-camera line. Kling turns a snap into a wave after ~2.2 s → cut right after. Don't slow footage.

## 5. ffmpeg edits and exports

- <b>Script:</b> <code>python3 &lt;batch&gt;/work/build.py &lt;spec.json&gt;</code>. Spec: <code>{"segments":[{"src","ss","to"}],"texts":[{"t","a","b","fs"?,"y"?}],"music","music_ss","noise"?,"vol"?,"out"}</code> (examples: work/dance\_tt.json, snap\_v4.json). Font: <code>/usr/share/fonts/truetype/sand-box/google/Poppins/Poppins-Bold.ttf</code>.
- <b>What it does:</b> each segment → <code>scale=-2:1920,crop=1080:1920,fps=30,setsar=1</code>, libx264 crf 12 fast, no audio → concat → drawtext (default fs 58, y 150, white, borderw 5 black, centred) + <code>noise=alls=&lt;noise, default 4&gt;:allf=t</code> → music <code>atrim</code> to length, 0.3 s fade-in, 0.6 s fade-out, volume 0.9 → final encode.
- <b>Final encode (required):</b> <code>-c:v libx264 -profile:v main -pix_fmt yuv420p -crf 19 -maxrate 14M -bufsize 28M -preset medium -threads 2 -r 30 -c:a aac -b:a 128k -ar 48000 -map_metadata -1 -movflags +faststart</code>. 1080x1920, <b>under 15 MB</b>.
- <b>Vertical framing:</b> always full-frame cover crop <code>scale=-2:1920,crop=1080:1920</code>, even for 3:4 sources. <b>Never letterbox, never blurred bands or padding</b> (Reviewer rule 10/22; supersedes the old blurred-canvas fin.sh). Never a non-uniform <code>scale=1080:1920</code> (stretched faces, -.05).
- <b>Text placement:</b> hook ~54 px, ending above ~y 240-350 (above her head); clear of TikTok/Shorts UI.
- <b>Verify:</b> <code>ffprobe -v error -show_entries stream=codec_name,profile,pix_fmt,width,height -show_entries format=size,duration &lt;out&gt;</code> → h264, Main, yuv420p, 1080x1920, size &lt; 15 MB; then re-gate the export (step 3) and <code>md5sum</code>. Thumb/cover: a no-text frame scoring ≥ .85, saved as <code>&lt;export&gt;_thumb.jpg</code> (YouTube) / <code>_cover.jpg</code>.
- <b>Fail → fix:</b> export fails the gate though raw passed → trim weak frames, move the in-point, raise headroom; &gt;15 MB → two-pass ~3.8 Mbps (lyric skill); <code>-metadata:s -1</code> errors in this ffmpeg → use <code>-map_metadata -1</code> only.
