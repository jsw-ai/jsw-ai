## Growth cadence (aggressive but safe) - added 2026-10-15
Second pass: what growth-focused sources/fast-growing creators do, vs. the conservative Buffer/Sprout averages above. Nothing was posted. Honest caveat: the evidence says MORE posts raises odds of a hit (Buffer 11.4M-post study: 11+/wk = up to +34% views/post vs 1/wk, but median video slightly worse), while a 58-creator TikTok sample (tokconnect.com, Sep 2026) shows heavy posters get FEWER views per video. So volume helps only if each post is distinct and decent; duplicates/near-identical posts are what trigger suppression. Ramp only if views/post and follows/post hold.

### Recommended per-day numbers for a NEW account (my synthesis, judgment)
Counts are published posts/day; "replies" = comments on others' posts (manual-style engagement). Space posts >=3-4h apart; every post unique (no cross-account duplicates, no repost-delete cycles).

| Platform | Week 1-2 warm-up | Week 3+ ramp | Never exceed (spam line) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram Reels | 1/day | 2/day (hold 1/day if reach drops) | 3/day; API cap 50/24h |
| Instagram carousels/photos | 0-1/day (4/wk) | 1/day | 2/day |
| Instagram Stories | 2-3/day | 4-6/day | ~10/day (cap 100) |
| TikTok | 1/day (2 on 2-3 days if content is ready) | 2-3/day, 4 only if views/post hold | 4/day (TikTok guidance), API 15/day |
| X original posts | 3/day | 5-8/day | 15/day (cap 50) |
| X replies | 10-15/day | 20-30/day | 50/day (cap 200) |
| YouTube Shorts | 1/day | 2/day, 3 once phone-verified & clean | 3-4/day; watch "daily limit" error |
| Facebook Reels | 1/day | 1-2/day | 3/day (API 30) |
| Facebook posts | 1/day | 1-2/day | 5/day |
| Reddit posts | 0 (comment-only 30d) | 0.15-0.3/day (1-2/wk/sub) | 2/day sitewide |
| Reddit comments | 3-5/day | 5-10/day | ~20-30/day, no duplicates |

Rules: (1) Week 1-2 also do manual engagement (see action limits above) and verify phone/email on every account. (2) Ramp by +1 post/day per platform per week only if no action blocks, no "try again later", and views/post >= prior week. (3) Any block/limit warning: halve cadence for 48h. (4) Judge by views per post and follows per post, not totals. (5) Owner's "far too little" view is partly right: sources do endorse 1-4 TikToks/day, 1-2 Reels/day and 3-8 X posts + 10-30 replies/day - but fast-growth evidence is that replies/engagement on X and variety of content, not raw post volume, drive follows.
