# Brand Name Ideas (checked 2026-10-12)

## FINAL DECISION (2026-10-12)
- **Brand: Gold Coast Babe**
- Domain: **goldcoastbabe.com** — unregistered as of 2026-10-12; to be bought during Shopify setup.
- One brand covers all products, including sun care.
- Ruled out:
  - Bondi Babe Co — conflicts with Bondi Babes (AU skincare TM) / Bondi Sands
  - Aussie Babe Co — live AU class-25 trademark
  - Bronzed Babe — registered swim brand
  - Coastal Babe Co — pending US COASTAL BABES filing

---
.com check method: Verisign RDAP (`rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/<name>.com`) — 404 = not registered, 200 = taken. Availability can change any minute and premium/aftermarket status isn't covered; confirm at registrar (🔒 owner).
Trademark: quick web search only — **not a USPTO/IP Australia clearance**. Notable finding: **"Sienna Swim" (siennaswim.com) is an established US swimwear brand that even sells a "Bondi" bikini set** → avoid "Sienna"+swim/beach names for the swim line.
