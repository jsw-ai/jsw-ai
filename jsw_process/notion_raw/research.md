Runs every Sunday. Output: one idea list per model for the coming week, saved in Notion, DMed to each model bot, summarized in the ops room. Never post or engage on any platform. Never mention or deny AI in captions.

## 1. Gather inputs (no ideas yet)

1. Ask each model bot for her day-by-day story for next week (location, events, travel). Ideas must follow that story.
2. Pull the Tracker's latest results: top hooks and formats per model and platform, credits per 1,000 views, and anything throttled or suppressed.
3. Check account status facts from the latest room and Tracker notes: which platforms are active, suspended, under appeal, or new; which models have a verified Fanvue (only verified models get Fanvue mentions, on X and Reddit only).
4. Re-read current rules in memory and the room, since they change often. Label anything unchecked as unverified.

## 2. Research live trends

1. Search current trending hooks, sounds, formats and lifestyle themes on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts and X (use X tools for live posts and trends).
2. Confirm any real event (game, holiday, weather, news) is real and current before using it. Never invent one.
3. Note sources on the research page.

## 3. Write each model's ideas

Per model, 2-3 posts a day across platforms, fitted to her story day by day. Each idea has: platform, hook (first-second line), format, setting, outfit, sound (checked in TikTok's commercial library for TikTok), caption angle, and a per-platform variant (different first second, hook text, crop or song; no identical file on two platforms).

- Content: sexy but SFW, exciting aspirational life (travel, pool, beach, gym, parties, games, first class, golf). No generic walking around.
- X: short clips are the core, plus threads and reply-bait text; single photos only now and then. Spicier tease (lingerie, bikini, implied) is X-only, no nudity. The Fanvue link lives in the bio and pinned post; captions use "link in bio" style, and only for verified models.
- TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and Facebook: clean brand content, no Fanvue mentions. Avoid dating or red-flag hashtags on TikTok. Instagram and TikTok get reels, not single stills.
- YouTube Shorts: name a thumbnail moment (clear face, peak of the hook) for each.
- Every model gets her own funny to-camera lip-sync bit, never copies.
- Reddit: only small subs that allow new accounts and selfies; note each sub's AI rule and that its rules must be screenshotted. No comment or karma plans.
- Weight toward formats the Tracker shows winning and that are cheap per 1,000 views.

## 4. Cross-model clash check

- No two models share a hook or trend that week.
- No two models post the same Shorts format on the same day (stagger lip-syncs and similar).
- No two models post gym on the same day on the same platform, and their gym looks differ.

## 5. Engagement banks

Refresh each model's comment and reply bank (TikTok and cross-platform) to match the week's story, following the warm-up pacing and follow plan pages.

## 6. Deliver

1. Save a research page plus one page per model under the JSW AI Notion home (Weekly Content Research - Week of \[date\]), plus a link to this process page.
2. DM each model bot her list and link.
3. Post a short summary with links in the JSW Operations room.
4. Report to JSW AI.
