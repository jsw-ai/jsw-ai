## 0. Non-negotiables (read first)
1. **Disclose AI everywhere.** Instagram, TikTok, Fanvue, Reddit all require or reward AI disclosure (details below). Never tell fans a persona is a real person. [V]
2. **Keep SFW and adult funnels separated.** Meta, TikTok, YouTube are mainstream/SFW-first; adult content lives on X (labeled), Reddit (AI-friendly NSFW subs) and Fanvue. [V]
3. **Fanvue requires real-person KYC** (government ID + liveness) even for AI accounts; the owner of the account must be a verified adult. [V]
4. **Never resemble a minor** (Fanvue explicit; universal risk). Avoid "teen/barely legal" language and youthful styling. [V]
5. **No real-person likeness** (celebrity lookalikes, face-swaps) without documented consent. [V]

## 3. Content calendar & cadence per platform
Evidence is thin for adult/AI personas specifically; benchmarks below are for general accounts.
- **Instagram**: 1–3 Reels/week associated with highest engagement in Dash Social's 700k+ Reels dataset. https://www.dashsocial.com/blog/how-often-should-you-post-on-instagram [V, general brand data]. Brand average cited: 3.7 IG posts/wk, 2.0 TikTok/wk (https://www.quid.com/knowledge-hub/resource-library/blog/2026-social-media-industry-benchmark-report [V]).
- **TikTok**: 2–5 videos/week; Buffer's 11.4M-post study found the biggest efficiency gain going from 1/wk to 2–5 (up to 17% more views per post). https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-should-you-post-on-tiktok/ [V]
- **X, Reddit, YouTube, Facebook cadence**: no verified benchmark found. **[U]** Starting hypothesis to test: X daily (several posts + replies); Reddit a few quality posts/week per persona across sub-appropriate communities, mostly comments; YouTube Shorts a few/week (repurposed TikTok/Reels but varied — see inauthentic-content rule); Facebook cross-post of Reels. Treat as experiments with a 4-week test window.
- **Per-persona weekly template [U]**: 2–3 IG Reels + stories, 3–4 TikToks (≥1 shoppable once Shop live), daily X (SFW teasers mixed with labeled adult posts for Sienna), Reddit participation daily, 1–3 Shorts, FB cross-post. Scale to 6 personas only as production capacity allows; prefer fewer, differentiated posts over cloned ones.
- **Content mix [U]**: ~lifestyle/SFW for IG/TikTok/YouTube/FB (reach platforms); spicier labeled content only on X/Reddit; paid/explicit only on Fanvue (and per its rules).

## 4. Analytics KPIs & review routine
Principle [V from Agelyra summary]: 5–10 KPIs tied to actions, not vanity metrics. https://agelyra.com/blog/creator-agency-weekly-operations-review/
**KPIs (suggested [U], definitions standard)**
- Reach/views per post, follower growth, engagement rate (saves/shares > likes), profile visits → link clicks (CTR), click → Fanvue signup conversion, free→paid sub conversion, ARPU/ARPPU, PPV unlock rate, tip frequency, churn/renewal, TikTok Shop GMV/commission/conversion, ban/warning count, % posts with correct label.
- Use tracked links per persona+platform (UTM or link-page analytics) so Fanvue signups map to source. [U]

**Daily (15–20 min) [U]**: check account health (warnings, reach drops, Account Status on IG), reply to comments/DMs, log any removal, glance at top/bottom post, confirm the day's posts went out with labels.
**Weekly (30–60 min) [V cadence]**: per-persona scorecard; top 3 / bottom 3 posts; funnel numbers (views→clicks→signups→paid); revenue by ladder rung; compliance sweep; decide: double down, fix, or stop; assign owners/dates.
**Monthly [U]**: persona-level go/no-go (cut or invest), cadence test results, review platform policy changes (policies above changed in 2024–2026).

## 5. Growth tactics per platform
**X (adult creator)** — evidence is mostly vendor blogs (low-to-medium reliability):
- Set account sensitive-media label; SFW avatar/banner/bio wording; adult posts can be excluded from search/recommendations but still reach followers/profile. (X policy [V]; reach suppression claims: https://peekin.bio/guide/x-twitter-bio-link , https://fanpull.io/guides/how-to-promote-fanvue-on-x [V as vendor claims, effect size [U]])
- Use a clean own-domain link page (18+ gate, analytics), not raw adult links in bio; pin a tracked high-converting post; consistent niche content, replies, genuine creator interaction; avoid repetitive/automated posting (spam/bot-in-bio risk). [vendor-sourced; treat as [U] until tested]
- Paid X ads cannot promote adult sexual content. https://iimagined.ai/blog/onlyfans-advertising-rules [vendor, [U]]
**Reddit**
- 90/10 (≤10% self-promo) is a rule of thumb; Reddit officially retired 9:1 in 2016 but many mods still enforce ~it; safest target cited: ~5% promo over trailing 90 days. https://signals.sh/blog/reddit-9-to-1-rule-how-to-track-your-ratio ; https://karmit.co/blog/how-to-promote-on-reddit [V — third-party blogs]
- Check each sub's rules + the "Create post" eligibility modal (account age/karma/verified email; exact thresholds hidden by Reddit). Third-party "typical" karma/age bands (https://www.soar.sh/blog/self-promotion-rules-by-subreddit-database) are observational [U].
- Use AI-friendly NSFW subs, label AI clearly, never use others' scripts/voices without permission (example: r/gonewildaiaudio rules https://www.reddit.com/r/gonewildaiaudio/comments/1uh4l2s/welcome_to_rgonewildaiaudio_aimade_spicy_audio/ [V]). Specific subs per persona [U — not researched].
- No multi-account vote/subscriber inflation, no bot promotion, no hidden affiliation, don't farm karma with recycled content. https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043504051-Spam [V]
**TikTok / Instagram reach**
- Cadence per §3; AI label on (IG "AI-generated profile"; TikTok toggle) — unlabeled risks lost recommendations on IG [V]. Hooks/trends/format specifics: not verified in this research [U].
**TikTok Shop**: hit 1,000 followers → apply (TikTok Studio > Monetization > TikTok Shop for Creator); expect 30-day pilot if <5,000 followers; identity verification means a **real, verified adult owner** is behind each account. Confirm that a disclosed AI persona is acceptable for product demos — policy forbids AI-fabricated product effects/deception, so real product footage is likely needed. [V rules; feasibility for AI personas [U]]
**YouTube / Facebook**: no persona-specific growth evidence gathered [U]. YouTube: vary content, add original value to avoid "inauthentic content".

## 6. Account warming & multi-account hygiene
Sources (vendor/tool blogs — medium-low reliability): https://support.post-bridge.com/troubleshooting/how-and-why-to-warm-up-a-new-tiktok-account-before-using-post-bridge ; https://help.blotato.com/social-accounts-and-platform-faqs/tiktok/brand-new-accounts ; https://docs.postonce.to/help/growth-guide/warm-up-accounts ; https://devicefarm.io/blog/how-many-accounts-per-phone ; https://unanswered.io/guide/does-tiktok-ban-you-for-having-multiple-accounts
- Spend ~1–4 weeks of natural in-niche activity (scroll, follow, like, comment, save) before heavy posting; post manually ~once daily first, then ramp gradually; avoid automation until warmed. [V — vendor guidance; exact durations are vendor opinion, [U] as platform fact]
- Avoid: mass follow/like, identical content across accounts, self-interaction between your own accounts, rapid account switching. [V vendor]
- Multiple accounts per phone are allowed but share device/IP/behavior signals; a violation can cascade to linked accounts. Prefer one persona per device/profile and a stable distinct IP. [V vendor; Meta/TikTok exact detection [U]]
- TikTok Shop explicitly bars sharing login info with high-violation shop accounts [V] — keep credentials per persona separate.
- Keep a per-persona asset/credential log (email, phone, 2FA owner) — recovery matters when a ban hits. [U]
- Fanvue allows linking up to 15 AI accounts under one verified identity and requires the first withdrawal on the original account. [V]
